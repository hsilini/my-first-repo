# Contributing Guide

Thank you for your interest in contributing to SmartBudgetManager!

## How to Contribute

1. Fork the repository.
2. Clone your fork to your computer.
3. Create a new branch for your changes.
4. Make your changes and test them.
5. Commit your changes with a clear message.
6. Push your branch.
7. Open a Pull Request describing your contribution.

## Commit Guidelines

- Use clear and descriptive commit messages.
- Keep commits focused on one task.
- Write clean and readable code.

## Reporting Issues

If you find a bug or have a suggestion, open an issue and describe it clearly.

Thank you for contributing!
